Problem

Phone baj raha hai, uthane wala koi nahi

Din ke 40 call aate hain. Aadhe uthte hi nahi — lunch ka time tha, do call ek saath aa gaye, ya banda counter pe customer ke saath busy tha.

Har miss hua call ek gaya hua customer ho sakta hai. Wo agli dukaan pe call karega aur wahin book kar lega. Aapko pata bhi nahi chalega ki kaun tha aur kya chahiye tha.

Aur jo utha raha hai — uski poori salary sirf phone uthane me nikal rahi hai. Apna kaam wo kar nahi pa raha, aur phone chhod bhi nahi sakta.

Toh sawaal ye nahi hai ki call zyada kaise aayein. Sawaal ye hai ki jo aa rahe hain, unka ho kya raha hai.

System

Paanch agents, paanch alag kaam

Har agent ek hi cheez karta hai. Ek hi. Isi wajah se pata rehta hai ki kaunsa hissa kaam kar raha hai aur kaunsa nahi.

  1. Agent 1

    Har call uthata hai

    Pehli ghanti pe. Hindi me, English me, ya customer jis zubaan me baat kar raha hai usi me. Poochhta hai kya chahiye, kab aana hai, aur slot book kar deta hai.

    Ek saath das call aayein to bhi das uthenge. Busy tone kisi ko nahi milti.

  2. Agent 2

    Sunta hai aur likh ke rakhta hai

    Call ke saath-saath summary banti jati hai — customer ne poochha kya tha, chahiye kya tha, kya tay hua. Call khatam, likha hua tayyar.

    Isi wajah se agli baar jab wo customer call kare, aapko yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi padti.

  3. Agent 3

    CRM sambhalta hai

    Booking, customer detail, appointment — sab apni jagah pe chala jata hai. Aur agar do log ek hi slot maang lein, to ye khud pakad leta hai aur doosre ko paas ka khaali time offer kar deta hai.

    Double booking ka phone baad me aapko nahi aata — kyunki wo hui hi nahi.

  4. Agent 4

    Follow-up karta hai

    Jisne appointment liya aur aaya nahi, usko WhatsApp pe message jata hai. Taana nahi — sirf ek line ki reminder aur dobara book karne ka option.

    Ye wahi kaam hai jo har koi karna chahta hai aur koi karta nahi, kyunki shaam tak yaad hi nahi rehta.

  5. Agent 5

    Hafte ki report deta hai

    Kitne call aaye, kitne book hue, kitne no-show, aur sabse zyada log kya poochh rahe hain. Wo aakhri wali cheez sabse kaam ki hai.

    Agar 30 log ek hi sawaal poochh rahe hain, to wo ek call ka issue nahi hai — wo aapke board ya page ka issue hai.

Kyun

Paanch chhote agents, ek bade voice bot ke bajaye

Sawaal wajib hai — ek hi voice bot se sab karwa lo na?

Karwa sakte ho. Do hafte theek chalega. Phir ye hoga: call to accha uthaya, par CRM me galat slot chala gaya. Ya summary theek likhi, par follow-up kisi ko gaya hi nahi. Aur aapko pata nahi chalega ki toota kahan hai, kyunki sab ek hi jagah ho raha tha.

Paanch alag agents ka fayda ye hai ki har ek ka kaam itna chhota hai ki usme galti karna mushkil ho jata hai. Agent 3 ko sirf slot dekhne hain — usse baat karna nahi aata, aur aana bhi nahi chahiye.

Aur jab kuch off lage — maan lo follow-up nahi ja rahe — to sirf Agent 4 ko theek karna hai. Baaki chaar chalte rehte hain, phone band nahi hota.

Ek voice bot ek feature hai. Paanch agents milke ek employee ban jaate hain — aur farak wahi hai.

Kharcha

Salary se compare karke dekhte hain

Ek receptionist ki salary 18 se 25 hazaar mahina hai. Wo 8 ghante baithta hai, ek time pe ek call uthata hai, chhutti leta hai, aur raat ko phone band rehta hai.

Ye paanch agents 24 ghante chalte hain, ek saath jitne call aayein utne uthate hain, aur chhutti nahi lete. Par ye software hai — walk-in customer ke saamne muskura ke chai offer karna isse nahi aayega. Wo abhi bhi aadmi ka kaam hai.

Cost do hisson me hoti hai: setup ek baar, aur uske baad mahina. Kitna — wo aapke call volume aur kitni cheezein jodni hain uspe depend karta hai, isliye main yahan ek number likh ke jhooth nahi bolunga. Poochhoge to seedha bata dunga.

Compare karne ka tareeka simple hai. Upar calculator me jo mahine ka number aaya — usse dekho. Agar mahine ki cost us number se kaafi neeche hai to baat banti hai. Nahi banti to mat karo, koi baat nahi.

Employee ek baar paid nahi hota — har mahine hota hai. Ye bhi wahi cheez hai. Isliye faisla mahine ke number pe hona chahiye, setup ke number pe nahi.
