Reel ka poora workflow

Ek inbox. Paanch agents. Kuch dabta nahi.

Reel me maine bola tha ki poora workflow bhej dunga. Ye raha — paanch agents kaun kya karta hai, aur uske neeche wo rulebook jisse har message chaar me se ek category me jata hai. Ye system maine apne hi brand ke liye chalaya hai, isliye jo cheezein practice me tootti hain wo bhi likhi hain.

Jo banta hai wo ye hai:Har message padha jata hai — text ho ya voice note. Har message ko ek category milti hai. Roz wale sawaalon ka jawab turant chala jata hai, baaki sahi bande tak pahunch jata hai, aur har cheez ek sheet me likhi jati hai. Mahine ke end me aapko pata hota hai ki log sabse zyada kya poochh rahe hain — aur wo apne aap me ek business insight hai.
Problem

Aapka asli office WhatsApp hai

Dukaan ka pata ab koi nahi poochhta. Sab WhatsApp pe aa jaate hain. Order bhi wahin, sawaal bhi wahin, shikayat bhi wahin, aur payment ka screenshot bhi wahin.

Dikkat ye hai ki chaaron ek hi list me girte hain. Order ke beech me kisi ka 'timing kya hai' aa gaya, uske neeche kisi ka screenshot, aur uske neeche wo banda jo teesri baar poochh raha hai ki maal kahan hai.

Aap upar se neeche padhte ho, teen ka jawab dete ho, chautha neeche khisak jata hai. Shaam tak wo 40 message neeche hai. Wo miss nahi hua — wo dab gaya, aur dabe hue message ka koi notification nahi aata.

Toh sawaal ye nahi hai ki jawab kaun dega. Sawaal ye hai ki inbox sort kaun kar raha hai.

System

Paanch agents, paanch alag kaam

Har agent ek hi cheez karta hai. Ek hi. Isi wajah se pata rehta hai ki kaunsa hissa kaam kar raha hai aur kaunsa nahi.

  1. Agent 1

    Sab kuch padhta hai

    Text bhi, voice note bhi. Voice note transcribe hoke text ban jata hai aur usi tarah aage badhta hai. Ye zaroori hai — aadha India type nahi karta, bolta hai, aur order aksar voice note me hi aata hai.

    Photo me likhi hui list ye nahi padhta. Us pe neeche order wale block me alag se likha hai.

  2. Agent 2

    Category deta hai

    Order, sawaal, shikayat, payment. Bas chaar. Ye agent jawab nahi deta — sirf label lagata hai, aur poora system usi label pe chalta hai.

    Jo chaaron me fit na ho — aur roz do-chaar hote hain — wo bina label ke seedha aapke paas jata hai. Agent 2 ko andaaza lagane ki permission nahi hai.

  3. Agent 3

    Roz wale sawaalon ka jawab deta hai

    Timing, address, delivery charge, payment kaise karna hai — inka jawab turant chala jata hai, raat ke barah baje bhi. Iske paas ek approved list hai: sawaal aur uska likha hua jawab.

    List me nahi hai to jawab nahi deta. Guess karna iske liye mana hai — aur yahi iski sabse kaam ki khoobi hai.

  4. Agent 4

    Sahi bande tak pahunchata hai

    Order dispatch wale ke paas, payment accounts ke paas, shikayat seedha owner ke paas. Message ke saath ek line bhi jati hai ki maamla kya hai, taaki bande ko poora chat scroll na karna pade.

    Route sirf naam nahi hota, usme ek time bhi hota hai. Do ghante tak kisi ne haath nahi lagaya to wo cheez upar aa jati hai.

  5. Agent 5

    Sab kuch sheet me likhta hai

    Har message ek row ban jata hai — kab aaya, kisne bheja, category kya lagi, auto-reply gaya ya nahi, kiske paas gaya, ab status kya hai.

    Ye hissa mahine ke end me kaam aata hai. '60 me se 22 log ek hi cheez poochh rahe the' — ye sirf sheet se dikhta hai, yaad se kabhi nahi.

Rulebook

Agent 2 ka triage rulebook

Ye wo hissa hai jo baaki sab decide karta hai. Category galat lagi to jawab galat jayega, banda galat pahunchega, aur sheet me galat likha jayega. Isliye har category ke liye chaar cheezein pehle se tay hain.

Seedha apni category pe jao:

  • Order
  • Sawaal
  • Shikayat
  • Payment

Har block me chaar cheezein hain, isi order me — Signal (pehchaan kaise hoti hai), Jawab (bot dega ya aadmi), Route (kiske paas jata hai), Log (sheet me kya likha jata hai).

Rulebook ka pehla block — order kaise pehchana jata hai aur uska rasta kya hai.

Order — auto-confirm kabhi nahi

Order sabse mehnga message hai. Dab gaya to paisa gaya, aur galat confirm ho gaya to usse bhi zyada gaya. Isliye is category me system tez hai par bolta bahut kam hai.

  • Signal Kaise pakadta hai

    Sabse pakka signal quantity hai — '2 dozen', '5 packet', '10 piece'. Uske saath koi kaam wala shabd: bhej do, chahiye, kar do, pack kar dena. Address ya pin code paste ho jaana bhi order hi hai. Akela 'rate kya hai' sawaal hai — par 'rate kya hai 10 packet ka' order ban chuka hai, kyunki quantity aa gayi.

  • Jawab Bot dega ya aadmi

    Bot sirf itna bolta hai ki message mil gaya aur confirm hoke aayega. Order confirm bot kabhi nahi karta — stock aur rate uske paas hai hi nahi. Ek baar bot ne 'ho jayega' bol diya aur maal tha nahi, to aapne ek dabe hue message ki jagah ek naaraz customer bana liya. Ye trade wapas nahi karna.

  • Route Kiske paas jata hai

    Jo dispatch ya billing dekhta hai, usi ke paas — WhatsApp pe bhi aur sheet me bhi. Do ghante tak kisi ne status nahi badla to row upar aa jati hai aur ek ping aapko jata hai. Order wahi ek category hai jiska timer sabse chhota rakha gaya hai.

  • Log Sheet me kya jata hai

    category = order, message jaisa aaya waisa ka waisa, auto_replied = yes (sirf acknowledgement gaya tha), assigned_to = jiske paas gaya, status = pending. notes me wo quantity aur item jo nikala gaya — taaki baad me dikh jaye ki sahi nikala tha ya nahi.

Yahan do cheezein practice me tootti hain. Ek — log order aadhe-aadhe me bhejte hain: upar product ka photo, neeche voice note me sirf '2 bhej do'. Akela message padho to kuch samajh nahi aata, isliye Agent 2 pichhle do-teen message saath me padhta hai. Ye rule baad me joda gaya tha, kyunki shuru me exactly yahi toot raha tha. Do — haath se likhi list ka photo. Wo abhi bhi aadmi hi padhega; system sirf 'list wali photo aayi hai, dekh lo' bol ke aage badha deta hai. Ye kami hai, chhupa nahi raha.

Poora rulebook upar se

Sawaal — sirf yahi ek category hai jisme bot khud jawab deta hai.

Sawaal — jawab list se aata hai, dimaag se nahi

Volume sabse zyada isi me hota hai aur mehnat sabse kam. Aksar 60-70% messages wahi das-baarah sawaal hote hain jo roz aate hain — aur roz koi aadmi haath se unka jawab likh raha hota hai.

  • Signal Kaise pakadta hai

    Sawaal wale shabd — kya, kaise, kab, kitna, kahan, hai kya — ya seedha '?'. Topic aksar ginti ke hote hain: timing, address, delivery charge, payment ka tareeka, size ya variant hai ya nahi. Question mark ka na hona koi matlab nahi rakhta, WhatsApp pe aadhe log '?' lagate hi nahi.

  • Jawab Bot dega ya aadmi

    Auto-reply, par sirf approved list me se. List me hai to jawab turant chala jata hai, chaubees ghante. List me nahi hai to Agent 3 jawab banata nahi — aadmi ke paas bhej deta hai. Ye limitation nahi hai, ye rule hai. Jis din bot ne apni taraf se rate ya delivery date bata di, us din us number pe bharosa khatam.

  • Route Kiske paas jata hai

    Jiska jawab ja chuka, wo kisi ke paas nahi jata — bas sheet me likh jata hai. Jo list me nahi tha wo sales ya aapke paas jata hai, ek line ke saath ki poochha kya gaya tha. Wahi list dheere-dheere badi hoti jati hai — aaj ka escalation agle mahine ka auto-reply hai.

  • Log Sheet me kya jata hai

    category = question, auto_replied = yes ya no, status = closed agar bot ne jawab de diya. Aur notes me topic tag — timing, price, delivery, stock. Ye tag chhota sa dikhta hai par yahi mahine ke end wali insight banata hai.

Sabse badi galti yahan ye hai ki har sawaal ko sawaal maan liya jaye. 'Delivery kab tak aayegi? 4 din ho gaye' — dikhne me sawaal hai, hai shikayat. Rule seedha hai: sawaal me agar time beet chuka ho ('4 din ho gaye', 'kal se wait kar raha hoon') ya kisi purane order ka zikr ho, to wo sawaal nahi hai — wo shikayat wale block me jayega. Ye ek rule sabse zyada bachata hai, kyunki uska ulta bahut mehnga hai: naaraz aadmi ko FAQ ka jawab chala jana.

Poora rulebook upar se

Shikayat — yahan speed se zyada zaroori hai ki wo kisi aadmi tak pahunche.

Shikayat — bot solve nahi karta, sirf aadmi tak pahunchata hai

Shikayat me nuksaan message dabne se nahi hota, der se hota hai. Banda already naaraz hai — do ghante ki chuppi usse review likhne bhej deti hai.

  • Signal Kaise pakadta hai

    Do tarah ke signal hain. Shabd wale — nahi aaya, galat aaya, toota hua, refund, wapas, ab tak nahi. Aur behaviour wale — wahi banda thodi der me teen message, purane order ka reference, ya time beet jaane wali line ('teen din ho gaye'). Behaviour wale zyada bharose ke hain, kyunki shabd badalte rehte hain aur behaviour nahi badalta.

  • Jawab Bot dega ya aadmi

    Bot sirf ek chhota acknowledgement bhejta hai jisme ek naam aur ek time hota hai — 'Rahul dekh raha hai, aaj shaam tak reply karega'. Solution bot kabhi nahi deta. Aur ye line jaan boojh ke chhoti rakhi gayi hai: naaraz aadmi ko lamba polite template bhejna aag me ghee daalna hai.

  • Route Kiske paas jata hai

    Owner ya senior ke paas — us bande ke paas nahi jisne wo order handle kiya tha. Wajah seedhi hai: apni hi galti wali shikayat log dabaate hain, jaan boojh ke nahi, bas 'main khud handle kar lunga' soch ke. Ye rule maine apne yahan seekha hai, kitaab se nahi.

  • Log Sheet me kya jata hai

    category = complaint, status = open. Ye row apne aap kabhi close nahi hoti — koi aadmi haath se close karega. notes me pehla message aur kya action liya gaya. Mahine me kitni rows open padi reh gayi, wo apne aap me ek report hai.

Sentiment pe mat jao. Hinglish me taana aur tareef bilkul ek jaise likhe jaate hain — 'wah bhai bahut badiya service' ko tone dekh ke koi bhi system positive maan lega. Isliye rulebook me tone ka weight jaan boojh ke kam hai aur behaviour ka zyada: kitni baar likha, kitna time beet gaya, purane order ka zikr hua ya nahi. Aur ek baat jo kisi rule me nahi aati — jo banda bilkul chup ho jaye, wo sabse badi shikayat hai. Usko koi system nahi pakdega. Wo aapko khud dhoondhna padega, aur sheet me purani closed rows dekhna hi uska ekmatra tareeka hai.

Poora rulebook upar se

Payment — yahan ek line hai jo system kabhi cross nahi karta.

Payment — screenshot milna aur paisa aana do alag baatein hain

Payment ka screenshot din me kai baar aata hai aur 90% cases bilkul sahi hote hain. Ye poora rulebook un baaki 10% ke liye likha gaya hai.

  • Signal Kaise pakadta hai

    Image ke saath chhota sa text — done, kar diya, paid, bhej diya, ho gaya. Ya baarah ankon wala UTR / transaction reference, jo aksar akela hi chipka hota hai. Kabhi sirf image aati hai, bina ek shabd ke — agar usse pehle order ki baat chal rahi thi to wo payment hi hai, aur yahi wo jagah hai jahan pichhle message padhna kaam aata hai.

  • Jawab Bot dega ya aadmi

    Bot bolta hai 'screenshot mil gaya, check karke confirm karte hain'. Ye kabhi nahi bolta ki payment mil gaya. Screenshot ka matlab paisa aana nahi hota — pending transaction ka screenshot successful jaisa hi dikhta hai, UPI reverse ho jati hai, banda galat UPI ID pe bhi bhej deta hai, aur screenshot banaya bhi ja sakta hai.

  • Route Kiske paas jata hai

    Accounts ya jo bhi bank statement se milata hai. Payment wali row usi sender ki order wali row se jodi jati hai, taaki dono ek saath dikhein — warna sheet me screenshot to ho jata hai par kis cheez ka tha, ye pata nahi chalta.

  • Log Sheet me kya jata hai

    category = payment, status = unverified. Verified tabhi likha jata hai jab kisi ne bank me dekh liya ho — system khud kabhi verified nahi likhta. notes me amount wo hi jo screenshot me dikha tha, saath me 'claimed' likh ke, kyunki wo padha hua number hai, mila hua nahi.

Ek cheez jo obvious lagti hai par hai nahi: system amount screenshot me se padh leta hai, aur usi ko sach maan lena hi asli galti hai. Isliye sheet me shabd hi 'claimed' rakha gaya — chhota sa farak hai, par mahine ke end me reconcile karte waqt yahi bachaata hai. Doosri cheez — 'paisa bhej diya' likh dene wale log jinke saath koi screenshot nahi aata. Wo payment nahi hai, wo dawa hai. Rulebook usko category to payment hi deta hai, par status unverified aur notes me 'screenshot nahi aaya' — taaki accounts ko pehle se pata ho ki dhoondhna padega.

Poora rulebook upar se
Kyun

Paanch chhote agents, ek bade inbox bot ke bajaye

Sawaal wajib hai — ek hi bot ko poora inbox de do na?

De sakte ho. Dikkat ye hai ki us bot ko ek hi saans me do kaam karne padte hain — samajhna ki ye hai kya, aur uska jawab dena. Aur jab wo confuse hota hai to chup nahi rehta, jawab de deta hai. Sabse mehnga failure yahi hai: shikayat ka jawab usne sawaal jaisa de diya. Banda naaraz tha, usko FAQ mil gaya.

Paanch alag agents me ye ho hi nahi sakta, kyunki jo pehchaanta hai (Agent 2) aur jo jawab deta hai (Agent 3) wo ek cheez hai hi nahi. Agent 3 ke paas approved list ke alawa kuch hai hi nahi — usse guess karna aata hi nahi, aur ye kami nahi, design hai.

Aur practical fayda ye hai: jis din lage ki auto-reply theek nahi ja rahe, aap sirf Agent 3 band kar dete ho. Baaki chaar chalte rehte hain — messages phir bhi padhe ja rahe hain, category phir bhi lag rahi hai, routing phir bhi ho rahi hai, sheet phir bhi bhar rahi hai. Inbox thoda dheema ho jata hai, band nahi hota.

Ek bade bot me 'thoda band karo' jaisa option hota hi nahi — wahan sirf on aur off hai. Paanch agents ka asli fayda ye hai ki inme ek agent band karna poore system ko band karna nahi hota.

Sheet

Log sheet — copy bana lo

Agent 5 isi me likhta hai. Aath column — timestamp, sender, category, message, auto_replied, assigned_to, status, notes. Upar rulebook me jo bhi 'sheet me kya jata hai' likha hai, wo inhi columns me jata hai.

Andar paanch example rows daali hui hain — chaaron category ki ek-ek, aur ek wo sawaal jo list me nahi tha aur aadmi ke paas gaya. Rows padh lo, format apne aap samajh aa jayega.

Sheet apne aap me bhi kaam ki hai. System na bhi lagao — aaj se ye chaar category haath se likhna shuru kar do. Mahine bhar me aapko khud dikh jayega ki sabse zyada kya aa raha hai, aur uske baad automate karne ka faisla asaan ho jata hai.

Aage

Apne inbox pe set karwana ho to

Rulebook as-is utha ke use kar sakte ho — categories zyadatar businesses me kaafi milti-julti hoti hain. Apne inbox pe set karwana ho to message kar do, aur do cheezein bata dena: din ke lagbhag kitne message aate hain, aur unme sabse zyada kya hota hai. Main dekh ke bata dunga ki aapke case me kitna auto ho sakta hai aur kitna aadmi ke paas hi rehna chahiye. Kaafi cases me jawab 'poora nahi' hota hai, aur wo bhi seedha bata dunga.
