Common Questions

How do I get started?

It starts with a short discovery call about how enquiries reach you today and where they're getting lost. Message me on WhatsApp to set one up — or have the demo agent call you first.

How long does a build take?

Design takes a couple of days and a typical first system is built and tested over two to four weeks, then watched closely through its first week live. Most of that window is testing — it's where the real edge cases surface. Systems that have to connect to awkward software take longer, and you'll hear that on the first call rather than halfway through. Full breakdown here.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions aren't really the unit here. During the build we keep tuning until the system behaves the way you signed off on in the design stage — that's the job, not an extra. Once it's live, changes are what the retainer covers, because a system that talks to customers needs adjusting for as long as it runs.

What are your payment terms?

Setup is invoiced against the build itself, and the retainer is billed monthly from the day the system goes live. The exact split is written into the proposal before any work starts, so there are no surprises mid-build. Payments via UPI, bank transfer, or international transfer for overseas clients.
