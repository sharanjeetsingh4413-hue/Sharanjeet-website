About

One person. That's the point.

I'm Sharanjeet Singh. I work solo out of Delhi, building AI voice agents, WhatsApp automation and the lead pipelines that connect them for businesses across India — and occasionally for clients in Dubai and Canada.

You might reasonably ask whether a one-person operation should be trusted with a system your leads depend on. Here's my case. You get the person who built it. When your agent says something odd on a Tuesday afternoon, you're not explaining it to an account manager who forwards it to someone who'll look at it next sprint — you're messaging the person who wrote the prompt and wired the integration, and who can change it that day. Nothing gets lost in translation between the person who sold it and the person who has to make it work, because they're the same person.

The tradeoff is honest: I take on a limited number of builds at a time, so if I'm full, I'll tell you rather than take your deposit and put you in a queue. And I document every system so it's yours — where it's wired, what each part does, where the accounts live. If you ever want to hand it to someone else, you can.

Why trust me with your phone line

Systems in production, not demos

The clinic agent is live and handling real calls right now — not a prototype, not a video of one. Anything I show you either runs today or is clearly labelled as still in build.

Built on what you already use

Your CRM, your sheets, your numbers, your calendar. I build around the tools your team already knows rather than asking a business to change how it works to suit my stack.

Hindi and English, as people actually speak

Built for how Indian customers talk on the phone — switching mid-sentence, talking over the agent, calling from a noisy street. That's what gets tested, because that's what happens.

Documented and transferable

You own the system: the accounts, the configuration, and a written record of how it's wired. If you ever want to move it to someone else, you can. No hostage-taking.
