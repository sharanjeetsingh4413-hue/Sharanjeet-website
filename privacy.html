Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 16, 2026

I respect your privacy and I’m committed to protecting the personal information you share with me. This Privacy Policy explains what information I collect, why I collect it, how I use it, and the choices you have. This policy applies to services provided by Sharanjeet Singh ("I", "me", "my") through this website and related communications.

1. Information I Collect

  • Contact information: name, email, phone number, and WhatsApp messages when you contact me for enquiries or quotes.
  • Project information: details you provide about your business and processes, and any account credentials you share so a system can be built or connected (CRM, WhatsApp Business, telephony, hosting). Sensitive credentials should be shared securely.
  • Call and message data: where I build or support a voice or messaging system for you, that system handles conversations with your customers. You are the controller of that data; I process it only to build, test, monitor and support the system on your instructions. Recordings, transcripts and message logs are held in the platforms your system runs on (for example the voice platform, WhatsApp Business API and your CRM), under those providers’ terms.
  • Payment information: transaction identifiers and payment metadata when you pay for services. Actual card/UPI details are processed by the payment provider (e.g., Razorpay) and are not stored on my servers.
  • Usage data: analytics, IP address, device and browser information, pages visited, and other standard web log data.
  • Cookies and tracking: cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the website and provide analytics.

2. How I Use Your Information

I use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communicate about projects.
  • Provide, deliver, and bill for services (including processing payments via third-party processors like Razorpay).
  • Improve this website and my services, and analyse usage patterns.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect my rights.

3. Payments & Razorpay

Payments on this site are processed by trusted third-party payment gateways such as Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal. I do not collect or store your full payment card details. I receive confirmation and limited payment metadata (transaction ID, amount, currency, and status) to reconcile payments and provide invoices. Any disputes about payment processing or chargebacks are handled per the payment provider’s policies and my Refund Policy.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

I may share personal information with:

  • Service providers who help deliver my services (payment gateways, hosting, analytics, email providers).
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect my rights.
  • Third parties in connection with a sale, merger or acquisition of the business (subject to confidentiality and data protection obligations).

5. Data Retention

I retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described (project delivery, billing, legal compliance). Project files and backups may be retained until you request deletion or per contractual terms.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact me using the details below. I may need to retain certain information for legal or business purposes.

7. Security

I use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal data. However, no online transmission or storage is completely secure — if you have sensitive information to share, contact me first to arrange a secure transfer.

8. Third-Party Links

This site may contain links to third-party sites. I am not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

9. Children

My services are not directed to children under 18. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
