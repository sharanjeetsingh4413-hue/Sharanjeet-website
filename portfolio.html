Aesthetic clinic · Greater Noida

An outbound agent that works through a backlog of old enquiries

The problem

Months of old enquiries were sitting in a spreadsheet. The front desk was busy with walk-ins and never got to calling them back.

What I built

  • An outbound voice agent on Bolna that calls dormant leads in Hindi and English
  • A connection to the clinic's CRM through n8n
  • IST scheduling that understands spoken dates and weekdays
  • Duplicate protection, so the same person is never called twice

What it does

Works through the backlog on a schedule, qualifies interest on the call, books the appointment into the CRM, and alerts staff for anyone who needs a human.

  • Bolna
  • n8n
  • CRM integration
  • Hindi + English

Status: Live in production

Bathroom & sanitaryware showroom · Delhi · Est. 1985

Phone-only enquiries moved onto WhatsApp, with a trail

The problem

A 40-year-old brand with no digital presence. Enquiries came by phone only and nothing was tracked, so the showroom's catalogue never reached anyone who hadn't walked in.

What I built

  • WhatsApp Business API setup
  • A content system for the product catalogue
  • Enquiry capture routed to the sales team

What it does

Product enquiries arrive on WhatsApp with context, get logged, and reach the right person instead of scattering across personal phones.

  • WhatsApp Business API
  • Content system
  • Lead capture
